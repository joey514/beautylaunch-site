What We Install

Growth Infrastructure.
Not Agency Busywork.

Ads are one part of six. The rest is what turns an enquiry at 9 pm into a patient on the table Thursday morning, and keeps them coming back.

01
Patient Acquisition Campaigns
Meta and Google campaigns built around your treatment mix and your margins, not a "$9 per unit" bait offer that fills the room with the wrong patient. Engineered for people who book, show up and buy the package.
Treatment-specific creative and targeting for your exact menu
Ad spend runs in accounts you own and can see
Optimised for cost per booked patient, not cost per lead
02
Speed-to-Lead Follow-Up
Every enquiry gets a text within minutes, seven days a week. A real person picks up the conversation the moment it needs one, so nobody who messages at 9 pm waits until Monday.
First reply in minutes, evenings and weekends included
Treatment, timing and budget qualified before your desk is involved
Handoff to a person the patient never notices
03
Booking & No-Show Recovery
Confirmations, reminders, reschedules and waitlist fills so a booked slot stays booked and an open one doesn't stay open. Every empty slot is revenue you already paid to acquire.
Reminder sequences that cut no-shows
Instant rebooking when someone cancels
Waitlist texts that fill same-week gaps
04
Offer Architecture & Buying Events
Dated, capacity-capped events like The Off-Season, our laser hair removal campaign that opens September 1 and closes October 31. Cash collected up front, and six to eight more visits bought with every patient.
Offers rebuilt from your published menu and margins
Capped at what your rooms can actually deliver
One buying event per quarter, run for you
05
Patient File Reactivation
Win-back sequences for the people who already trust you. The cheapest patient to book is the one who was on your table last year, and most clinics have hundreds of them sitting in the file.
Lapsed-patient sequences by treatment and timing
Anniversary and seasonal reminders
Usually live, and rebooking, before a dollar of ad spend

Reviews & Reputation
Review requests timed to the treatment, not the invoice. Unhappy feedback routed to you before it's routed to Google, so your rating compounds the same way your calendar does.
Post-visit requests sent when results show
Negative feedback intercepted privately
Reviews reused in ads and follow-up
